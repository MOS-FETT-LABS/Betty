# Legal
## OpenCode
Betty sends prompts from the Pebble phone app to OpenCode (opencode.ai) using the OpenCode Go API key you configure. Betty does not provide a hosted helper service.
OpenCode Go use is subject to OpenCode's terms at https://opencode.ai/terms.
Optional Android enhanced mode may route assistant processing through the Android companion when available.
